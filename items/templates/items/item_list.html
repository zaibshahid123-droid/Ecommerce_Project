Frequently Asked Questions

How fast does in-villa resort delivery take? +

All dishes are prepared fresh to order in our central resort kitchen. Average preparation and delivery to your villa or table takes between 25 to 40 minutes in temperature-preserving hot cases.

Can we customize spice levels or request gluten-free options? +

Yes! You can specify your desired heat profile (Mild, Medium, Royal Spice) or dietary requirements directly during checkout or by mentioning it to the dining concierge.

What payment methods are supported? +

We support both Cash on Delivery (COD) upon receiving your order and Credit / Debit Card billing at checkout or room-charge for resort guests.
